## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SOURCE DOCUMENT LIMITATION DISCLOSURE

### 1.1 Scope of Forensic Audit
Following the successful completion of **Phase 1** (Core RBAC, Meat Attributes, Delivery Settings, CRM Notes), **Phase 2** (Flash Deals, Abandoned Carts, Customer Segmentation, Category SEO), **Phase 3** (Custom Meta Ads Manager, Meta Pixel, Conversions API, ROAS Attribution), **Phase 4** (Flexible Units, Product Availability Scheduling, Multi-Store Inventory, POS Readiness), **Phase 5** (Subscriptions, Loyalty Rewards, Referral Engine, Smart Reorder, Recipes, Meal Combos), **Delivery Slot & Dynamic Pricing Management**, and the resolution of all **10 Confirmed Bugs** (`MEATYNS_BUG_FIX_REPORT.md` and `MEATYNS_POST_FIX_TEST_REPORT.md`), a comprehensive, read-only forensic audit was performed across the entire Meatyns project.

The purpose of this audit is to assess the active codebase against the original client specification document:
**"MEATYNS Online Application + Website Development Requirement Document"**.

### 1.2 Source Document Limitation Disclosure
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Source Document Disclosure:**  
> A standalone binary document (`.pdf` or `.docx`) was not located in the repository root or developer workstation directories. However, the complete 19-section, 201-requirement specification from the client document was forensically extracted and preserved verbatim in the foundational repository audit `MEATYNS_REQUIREMENT_IMPLEMENTATION_AUDIT.md`.  
> 
> In accordance with the audit mandate, this audit does not substitute any alternate requirements. The 19 sections, requirement IDs (`PS-001` through `ARCH-003`), and requirement names are used as the immutable ground truth for this evaluation.

### 1.3 High-Level Findings & Codebase Reality
1. **Commercial Core Engine (100% Robust):** The atomic order creation, double-entry financial ledger (`LedgerEntry`), multi-store dark-store routing, distance-based delivery pricing, slot capacity engine, wallet redemption, and Razorpay HMAC signature verifications are functioning cleanly.
2. **Security Hardening (P0 Resolved):** The unconditional mock delivery OTP bypass (`BUG-01`) has been eliminated. Mock OTP `"1234"` is strictly rejected in production environments via `isMockDeliveryOtpPermitted()`, and all handoffs require active, unconsumed, unexpired OTP records within attempt limits.
3. **Advanced Growth & Marketing Systems (Substantially Built):**
   - **Custom Meta Ads Manager Suite:** 100% coded across 6 dedicated admin tabs, official OAuth 2.0 flow, PAUSED campaign draft state machine, client-side Meta Pixel, server-side Conversions API (CAPI) with SHA-256 PII hashing, 30-day order attribution, and Blended ROAS analytics. (Awaits live Meta credentials for external verification).
   - **Retention & Growth:** Recurring subscriptions with background job scheduling (`subscriptionOrderJob.js`), automatic delivery loyalty cashback (`loyaltyService.js`), customer referral codes with WhatsApp sharing (`referralService.js`), 1-click smart reorder, culinary recipes with 1-click ingredient-to-cart, and meal prep combos are implemented and operational.
4. **Automated Test Suite Status:**
   - **Backend:** **60 passed, 4 skipped out of 64 suites (357 passing tests, 22 skipped tests)**.
   - **Frontend:** **3 passed out of 3 suites (20 passing tests)**.
   - **Frontend Production Build:** **Compiled in 12.86s across 198 chunks with 0 compilation errors**.
   - **Skipped Suites:** The 4 skipped suites require a live MongoDB Atlas connection (`RUN_E2E_TESTS=true`) and are intentionally bypassed during offline unit runs.

## 8. SECURITY, RESILIENCE & TECHNICAL RISK ANALYSIS

1. **Delivery OTP Security (RESOLVED):**
   - *Previous Risk:* Hardcoded `"1234"` mock OTP allowed arbitrary delivery completion without validating database OTP records.
   - *Remediation Status:* Completely resolved. `isMockDeliveryOtpPermitted()` strictly returns `false` in production. Real OTPs are hashed using SHA-256 and verified against unconsumed, unexpired records within 3 attempts.
2. **Payment Tampering Protection:**
   - Cryptographic HMAC SHA-256 webhook signature verification prevents fake payment capture events.
   - Authoritative backend pricing recalculation in `hydrateOrderItems` prevents client-side price manipulation.
3. **Admin Credential & Token Protection:**
   - Meta access tokens stored in `MetaIntegration` with `select: false` and stripped in `toSafeDTO()` prevents leakage to frontend clients.
   - Staff passwords hashed using bcrypt with work factor 10.
4. **Data Isolation & Concurrency:**
   - Multi-seller cart isolation ensures independent order numbers and financial settlements.
   - Two-phase stock reservation decrements inventory atomically within MongoDB transactions.
